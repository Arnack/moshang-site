Privacy policy

Last updated: 30 October 2026

墨声 is built to work without collecting anything about you.

Microphone. When you tap the microphone, audio is analysed on your device to measure the pitch of your voice. Recordings are kept in memory only for playback during that check and are discarded afterwards. Audio is never uploaded, stored or shared.

Speech recognition (optional). If you turn on *Word recognition*, your device's built-in speech service (Apple, Google or your browser) transcribes what you said. That service is operated by your device's platform provider under its own privacy policy. It is off by default on phones.

Your progress. Saved words, reviews, scores, streaks and settings are stored only on your device. You can export them to a file yourself, and delete them any time from Settings → Reset progress, or by deleting the app.

Purchases. Purchases are processed by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play); we never see your payment details. On iPhone, the Lifetime purchase is verified through RevenueCat, which receives your App Store purchase receipt and a random, anonymous app user ID — no name, email or device advertising ID. See revenuecat.com/privacy.

Notifications. Daily reminders are scheduled locally on your device. No push server is involved.

No tracking. No analytics, advertising identifiers, cookies or accounts. RevenueCat (above) is the only third-party service, used solely to verify purchases.

Children. The app collects no personal data from anyone, including children.
