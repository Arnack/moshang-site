How does the tone check work?

When you tap the microphone, 墨声 measures the pitch of your voice many times a second, splits it into syllables and compares each one with the shape of the tone it should have. It all happens on your phone; audio is never uploaded.

The app keeps hearing the wrong tone

Open Settings → Your pitch range → Calibrate, and say the two syllables. Speak in your normal voice, about 20–30 cm from the phone, in a quiet room. Very fast speech and whispering are hard to read.

The microphone doesn't work

iPhone: Settings → 墨声 → Microphone. Android: Settings → Apps → 墨声 → Permissions → Microphone.

I don't hear any audio

Check the ring/silent switch and volume. Words without a bundled recording use your phone's Chinese voice — on iPhone you can download a better one in Settings → Accessibility → Spoken Content → Voices → Chinese.

Restore my Lifetime purchase (iPhone)

Open the app → Settings → Restore purchase, signed in with the Apple ID you bought it with. Lifetime is shared with your Family Sharing group.

Refunds

Purchases are handled by Apple and Google: request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com or through Google Play.

Moving to a new phone

Settings → Back up progress → Export, then Import on the new phone.
